# Surface brief: index.html (blog landing)

Mode: Read. One long page, Spanish, blog for adolescents (13–15); teacher verifies it exists. Five topics: Comer, Moverse, Dormir, Sentir, Pantallas y redes. Interaction: per-topic mini quiz that pastes the topic's sticker into the album on a correct answer. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, publishable on GitHub Pages/Netlify, credits exact (see PRODUCT.md), only sourced general facts. Anti-goals: album taking over the reading column; childish look; invented stats or people.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The blog is a sticker album; every healthy habit is a numbered lámina you earn by reading and pasting. Refuses the category default of photo hero + card grid of topics.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned palette #164E45 / #55BFA6 / #F5F8F2 / #FF806F. Album-page green fields own the frame (cover, section openers in two greens, quiz panels); reading column sits on calm off-white. Components: numbered dashed slots, white-bordered stickers slightly rotated, a flat-printed special sticker (coral star, 5/5) for completion, team ficha card, wide uppercase display (Anybody) over Figtree body (adaptation: Atkinson Hyperlegible was tried first, but its slashed zero renders Spanish figures as «4Ø0 gramos» in running prose, hurting the Readable-first principle). State is a mark, not a hue: empty numbered slot with a faint silhouette of its art, pasted sticker, special sticker. Every sticker prints its key fact and source; facts live in the prose, not in stat panels.

STORY: The reader sees an album to fill, reads each topic in a calm column, answers one question per topic and pastes the sticker; they leave with five habits and the album shows what they kept.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green cover. Top bar: album logo left, numbered topic nav right. Left ~65%: giant uppercase title "Vida saludable / en la adolescencia" (second line coral), one-line lead, team ficha (students, INSTICALDAS 8A, teacher). Right ~35%: 3×2 grid of album slots showing progress (pasted vs empty numbered) and the count "x de 5 láminas"; primary action "Abrir el álbum" under the lead.

FORM: Sticker album (álbum de láminas), position 4 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:15b849f303180d08
Raises: state as mark not hue (cutting bench); source on every sticker (provenance ribbon); achromatic reading column, color in frame only (iridescent cloud); pasted stickers persist as history (club sleeve).
