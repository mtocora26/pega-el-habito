# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework, no build step), published online so it can be shared with a link (e.g. GitHub Pages or Netlify; exact host not yet decided).

## Users

- **Evaluator:** Juan Carlos Isaza, Informática teacher at INSTICALDAS. He opens the page to check that the students built it; he does not need to be persuaded of anything.
- **Imagined reader (design target):** adolescents around 13–15 years old reading a health blog. The page is designed as if it were a real blog for them, even though its practical audience is the teacher.

## Product Purpose

A school web project for the Informática class, grade 8A, INSTICALDAS. Topic: "Vida saludable en la adolescencia" (healthy living in adolescence). It works like a small blog that explains healthy habits to teenagers. Success means the page is complete, works and looks professional, and clearly credits its authors.

## Positioning

Written for teenagers by teenagers: the tone is peer-to-peer, not that of a doctor or an advertisement.

## Operating Context

Opened in a browser through a public link, on a computer or a phone. Read once, not used every day.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Spanish.
- Required credits, exactly as written: students **María Isabel Castro Tocora** and **María José Puentes Ballesteros**; grade **8A**; school **INSTICALDAS**; subject **Informática**; teacher **Juan Carlos Isaza**.
- Must stay simple enough for 8th-grade students to understand and explain (plain HTML/CSS/JS files).
- Health facts must come from reliable sources (e.g. WHO/OMS) and name the source; no invented statistics or medical advice.

## Brand Commitments

Name: **Pega el Hábito** (chosen by the students; plays on pasting a sticker and making a habit stick). The assigned topic «Vida saludable en la adolescencia» stays as the page's main title/subtitle.

Palette chosen by the user (binding): dark natural green `#164E45`, turquoise `#55BFA6`, green-tinted white `#F5F8F2`, coral `#FF806F`. Desired feel stated by the user: modern, youthful, professional; conveys wellbeing, energy, freshness, calm and youth; avoid childish looks and overly loud colors.

## Evidence on Hand

No content from the students yet: no texts, photos or teacher requirements. Content is written from general, cited sources. Do not invent testimonials, student quotes, photos of real people or survey results.

## Product Principles

1. Credible: every health claim is general, sourced and safe for minors.
2. Readable first: it is a blog, so comfortable reading beats decoration.
3. Peer voice: talk to teenagers as equals, never preach or talk down.
4. Explainable: code the students can open, understand and present.
